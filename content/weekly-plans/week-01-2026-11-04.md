**04 – 07 Nov 2026 · rabu–sabtu · 3 post/hari · 12 post**
Sumber arc: ebook *AI for your daily life*. Satu post = satu micro-chapter.
Dipangkas dari 15 → 12 karena start hari Rabu. 6 topik masuk bench untuk week 02.

## rabu 04 nov — unlearn

**ch.01 · 09:00 · hot take**
> everyone asks AI to do more. i asked it what to delete.
> monday went from 19 things to 3. the other 16 were proof, not work.

**ch.02 · 13:00 · prompt card**
> the 3-musts calendar prompt
> "look at my calendar for today. keep only 3 that actually need me. the rest can stay soft."
> before: 11 things, none finished. after: 3, all done by 2pm.

**ch.03 · 20:30 · confession**
> my calendar looked busy but nothing felt important.
> busy was never the point. i was just scared of an empty square.

## kamis 05 nov — the yes-man

**ch.04 · 09:00 · hot take**
> AI agrees with everything you say. that makes it the most dangerous yes-man in your life.

**ch.05 · 13:00 · prompt card**
> one line to add to every prompt: "tell me where this is wrong."
> it stops being a mirror and starts being a second opinion.

**ch.06 · 20:30 · confession**
> i had 11 AI apps. i use 2. the other 9 were anxiety with a subscription.

---

## jumat 06 nov — consumption → action

**ch.07 · 09:00 · hot take**
> my pinterest has 200 saves and 0 actions. that's not a moodboard, that's a graveyard i keep feeding.

**ch.08 · 13:00 · prompt card**
> save → action in 10 minutes
> "turn this save into 1 tiny thing i can do in 10 mins with what i already own."
> "soft morning desk" → coffee without phone, 2 tabs only.

**ch.09 · 20:30 · confession**
> every sunday i ask AI one question: what did i do this week that i don't remember doing?
> it's been brutal. and the most useful 5 minutes of my week.

---

## sabtu 07 nov — context + off-ramp

**ch.10 · 09:00 · hot take**
> AI can write your essay but it can't tell you what to wear. it doesn't know you own 7 black shirts.
> context beats model. always.

**ch.11 · 13:00 · prompt card**
> the 5-line context dump — siapa kamu, kerjaan kamu, apa yang kamu ulang tiap hari, apa yang bikin kamu macet, apa yang kamu hindari.
> paste di awal tiap chat baru. semua prompt jadi lebih tajam.

**ch.12 · 20:30 · confession**
> closing the laptop doesn't close the simulation. there is no off-ramp.
> so i built one. it's 20 minutes, no screens, and it's the only reason i sleep.
